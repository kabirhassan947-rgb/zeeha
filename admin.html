How to update your shop: 1) Add or change products below. 2) Click Download products.json. 3) On GitHub, open your repository, click Add file > Upload files, drop in the new products.json (and any new photos into the images folder), then click Commit changes. Your shop updates in about a minute. Photos: write the file path, for example images/noor-1.jpg, one per line.
