About Us

Zeeha is a Nigerian modest fashion brand founded in 2021 by Zainab Haaleem Hassan. The brand was born out of a passion for creating elegant, comfortable, and timeless ready-to-wear pieces for women who appreciate modesty and style.

At Zeeha, we believe that fashion should be a balance of comfort, quality, and elegance. Our pieces are thoughtfully designed with beautiful fabrics, refined styles, and attention to detail, creating outfits that can be worn confidently on different occasions. We embrace simplicity and sophistication, with designs that reflect our commitment to making modest fashion effortlessly stylish.

Zeeha represents a love for fashion that goes beyond trends, focusing on pieces that make women feel confident, comfortable, and beautiful. Our aim is to continue creating quality ready-to-wear collections that celebrate femininity and make timeless modest fashion a part of every woman’s wardrobe.
